### 🚀 About Me

I am a Computer Engineering researcher focused on RTL design, FPGA prototyping, digital hardware, and hardware–software co-design for edge AI systems. I work with Verilog/SystemVerilog, Python, C/C++, simulation, verification, and hardware optimization. My interests include efficient accelerators, in-memory computing, HD computing, and designing practical hardware for real-world intelligent systems.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **A constraint-aware optimization framework for efficient edge-AI and in-memory-computing hardware**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **RTL verification, clock-domain crossing, FPGA implementation, timing analysis, and hardware acceleration**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **Open-source RTL projects, FPGA accelerators, edge-AI hardware, and hardware–software co-design**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **SystemVerilog, RTL design, FPGA prototyping, Verilator, digital logic, edge AI, and in-memory computing**  
😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **he/him**
